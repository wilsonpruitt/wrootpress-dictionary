(Greek *Aalar*), a person who (or a place from which some of the Jews) returned after the
captivity (1 Esdras v, 36); more correctly called in the parallel list (Neh. vii, 61) Immer
(q.v.).

<div class="editorial-note">

**Editorial Note (Wroot Press, 2026):** M&S leaves the person-or-place question open, but the
parallel list in Ezra 2:59/Nehemiah 7:61 is a list of Babylonian locations that returning exiles
could not trace their genealogy back to (Tel-melah, Tel-harsha, Cherub, Addan, and Immer) — a
place-name convention, not a list of ancestors. On that pattern Immer/Aalar is best read as a
place, not a person, resolving the ambiguity M&S's source (1 Esdras, a looser Greek paraphrase of
Ezra-Nehemiah) left unclear.

</div>
